Community Guidelines

Hostel Rules & Resident Policy

Simple, transparent guidelines designed to ensure safety, mutual respect, and a peaceful living atmosphere for every resident.

Timings & Biometric Entry

  • 24/7 biometric access for working professionals with night shifts.
  • Student wing main gate closes at 10:30 PM; prior warden intimation required for late return.
  • Valid Government ID & College/Company ID mandatory during check-in.

Visitors & Parents Policy

  • Visitors & parents are welcome in the Ground Floor Reception Lounge between 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
  • Visitors must sign the security register with a valid photo ID.
  • Overnight stay of non-residents inside rooms is strictly prohibited.

Cleanliness & Maintenance

  • Keep personal belongings organized so housekeeping can clean floors daily.
  • Switch off ACs, geysers, and lights before leaving the room.
  • High-wattage personal heaters or induction stoves are not permitted inside rooms.

Notice Period & Rent Payment

  • Monthly rent is payable on or before the 5th of every calendar month via UPI/Bank Transfer.
  • 30 days prior written notice is mandatory before vacating the PG.
  • 100% refundable security deposit settled within 48 hours of room handover.

Noise, Safety & Zero-Tolerance Conduct

Quiet hours are strictly observed from 10:30 PM to 7:00 AM across all corridors and rooms.
HavenStay is a strictly smoke-free, alcohol-free, and substance-free campus.
Use headphones for music/gaming after 10:00 PM to respect roommates.
Any damage to furniture or fixtures will be deducted at actual repair cost.
